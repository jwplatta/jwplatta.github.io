I'm Joe Platta. This site is where I publish research notes on topics I'm actively exploring -- primarily quantitative finance, options trading, machine learning, and software engineering.

The notes here are works in progress. They document what I'm learning, building, and testing, often in real time. Some are polished; many are not. The goal is to think clearly by writing, and to leave a trail that might be useful to others working on similar problems.
